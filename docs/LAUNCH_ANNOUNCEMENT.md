### What is HoloKai?

HoloKai is a multi-modal knowledge platform that combines:
- **Voice-First Interaction**: Ask questions naturally, receive spoken responses in authentic ancient voices
- **Visual Intelligence**: Generate artifacts, maps, and visualizations of historical events
- **Deep Knowledge Base**: Access comprehensive information about ancient civilizations, cultures, and innovations
- **Intelligent Archive**: Upload, organize, and search your own documents with AI-powered insights
- **Research Tools**: Conduct semantic search across vast historical databases

### Key Features

#### 🎙️ Voice Oracle
- Speak your questions naturally
- Receive responses in historically accurate voices (Egyptian scholars, Roman historians, etc.)
- Real-time transcription and synthesis
- Multi-language support

#### 👁️ Vision Engine
- Generate historical artifacts and visualizations
- Create maps of ancient civilizations
- Visualize architectural reconstructions
- AI-powered image analysis

#### 📚 Knowledge Archive
- Upload and organize documents
- AI-powered metadata tagging

#### 🔬 Research Suite
- Semantic search across 16 volumes of ancient knowledge
- Cross-reference civilizations and time periods
- Generate comprehensive reports
- Citation-ready outputs
