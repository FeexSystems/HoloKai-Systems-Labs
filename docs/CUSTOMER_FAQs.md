# HoloKai Customer FAQs

## General Questions

### What is HoloKai?

HoloKai is an AI-powered platform that brings ancient historical knowledge to life through voice interaction, visual intelligence, and deep knowledge retrieval. You can ask questions about ancient civilizations, receive spoken responses in historically accurate voices, generate visual artifacts, and build your own knowledge archives.

### How is HoloKai different from ChatGPT?

While ChatGPT is a general-purpose AI assistant, HoloKai is purpose-built for historical knowledge with:
- A curated knowledge base of 16 volumes covering ancient civilizations
- Specialized AI agents for knowledge, voice, vision, and archive tasks
- Voice-first interaction with historically accurate personas
- Visual generation of historical artifacts
- Document upload and semantic search
- Citation-ready outputs for academic use

### Is HoloKai free?

HoloKai offers a Free tier with limited features (50 voice queries/day, 100MB document storage). The Pro tier ($29/month) unlocks unlimited voice queries, full knowledge base access, and advanced features. The Enterprise tier ($199/month) adds team collaboration, API access, and dedicated support.

### How accurate is the historical information?

Accuracy is our top priority. Our knowledge base is curated by historians and subject matter experts, with multiple source verification for all information. We provide citations for responses and regularly update based on latest research. We transparently indicate uncertainty when information is ambiguous.

## Account & Billing

### How do I create an account?

Visit [holokai.systems](https://holokai.systems) and click "Sign Up". You can sign up with email/password or use Google/Facebook authentication. After signing up, you'll receive a confirmation email to verify your account.

### How do I upgrade my subscription?

Log in to your account, go to Settings > Subscription, and select your desired tier. You'll be prompted to enter payment information. Changes take effect immediately.

### Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes, you can cancel anytime from your account settings. Your access continues until the end of your current billing period. No refunds are provided for partial months, but you can continue using the Free tier after cancellation.

### What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover) and PayPal. Enterprise customers can pay via invoice with NET 30 terms.

### Do you offer refunds?

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee for new Pro and Enterprise subscriptions. After 14 days, refunds are not provided, but you can cancel to prevent future charges.

## Voice Oracle

### How does the Voice Oracle work?

The Voice Oracle uses speech recognition to transcribe your questions, routes them to specialized AI agents, generates responses using our knowledge base, and synthesizes the response in a historically accurate voice persona.

### What voice personas are available?

We offer voice personas including:
- Egyptian Scholar
- Roman Historian
- Greek Philosopher
- Mesopotamian Scribe
- Chinese Imperial Scholar
- Indian Vedic Teacher
- And more

Pro and Enterprise tiers can request custom voice personas.

### Can I use Voice Oracle in multiple languages?

Yes, Voice Oracle supports multiple languages including English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, and more. The system auto-detects your language and responds accordingly.

### Why isn't my voice input working?

Common solutions:
- Check microphone permissions in your browser
- Ensure your microphone is not muted
- Try a different browser (Chrome recommended)
- Close other apps that might be using the microphone
- Check your internet connection

### How accurate is the transcription?

Our transcription is 95%+ accurate on clear audio with minimal background noise. Accuracy may decrease with strong accents, background noise, or poor microphone quality.

## Vision Engine

### What can the Vision Engine create?

The Vision Engine can generate:
- Historical artifacts and objects
- Maps of ancient civilizations
- Architectural reconstructions
- Battle formations
- Cultural scenes
- And more based on your descriptions

### How long does image generation take?

Most images generate within 10-30 seconds. Complex requests or high-resolution outputs may take longer. Generation time depends on server load and query complexity.

### Can I use generated images commercially?

Pro and Enterprise tier users have commercial rights to generated images. Free tier users can use images for personal use only.

### Why did my image generation fail?

Common reasons:
- Query too complex or ambiguous
- Account tier limits reached
- Server temporarily unavailable
- Content policy violation (e.g., modern weapons, inappropriate content)
- Try simplifying your request or rephrasing

### Can I edit generated images?

Currently, you can regenerate with modified prompts. We're working on direct image editing features for future releases.

## Knowledge Archive

### What file types can I upload?

We support PDF, DOC, DOCX, TXT, MD, and image files (JPG, PNG). For best results, use text-based formats rather than scanned images.

### How much storage do I get?

- Free tier: 100MB
- Pro tier: 10GB
- Enterprise tier: Unlimited

### How does automatic metadata tagging work?

Our AI analyzes your document content and automatically tags it with relevant topics, time periods, civilizations, and content types. You can also add custom tags manually.

### Can I share documents with others?

Enterprise tier users can share documents with team members and set access permissions. Pro and Free tiers are for personal use only.

### Is my data private?

Yes, your documents are encrypted at rest and in transit. We never use your documents for training without explicit consent. Enterprise customers can choose data residency options.

## Technical Issues

### What browsers are supported?

We recommend Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge (latest versions). Internet Explorer is not supported.

### What are the system requirements?

- Modern browser with JavaScript enabled
- Stable internet connection (5 Mbps recommended)
- Microphone for voice input
- Speakers or headphones for voice output
- For best experience, use a device with at least 4GB RAM

### Why is the platform slow?

Performance issues may be caused by:
- Slow internet connection
- High server load during peak hours
- Browser with many open tabs
- Outdated browser version
- Try closing other tabs or using a different browser

### How do I report a bug?

Click "Help" > "Report Bug" in the platform, or email support@holokai.systems with:
- Description of the issue
- Steps to reproduce
- Browser and device information
- Screenshots if applicable

## Privacy & Security

### Is my voice data stored?

Voice data is processed temporarily for transcription and response generation. We do not store your voice recordings unless you explicitly save conversations to your history.

### Is my conversation history private?

Your conversation history is private and encrypted. Only you can access it. Enterprise admins can view team conversations if configured.

### Are you GDPR compliant?

Yes, HoloKai is fully GDPR compliant. EU customers can request data export or deletion under GDPR rights.

### Do you sell my data?

No, we never sell your data to third parties. Your data is used only to provide and improve our services.
