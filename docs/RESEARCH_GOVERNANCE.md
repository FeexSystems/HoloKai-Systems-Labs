# HoloKai Research Governance

Source metadata should capture primary/secondary status, archival/community origin, language, translation lineage, digitization lineage, rights, cultural protocol, editorial changes, corroboration and known bias.

Conflicting sources must remain explicitly conflicting rather than being flattened into false consensus.

Oral and indigenous knowledge should retain attribution, language, knowledge-holder/community context, access rules and cultural protocols.

Colonial archive material should carry provenance and bias annotations so the Oracle can distinguish administrative records, community oral records, archaeological evidence and later historiography.
