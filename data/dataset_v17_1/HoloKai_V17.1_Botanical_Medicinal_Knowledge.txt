**Version:** 17.1\

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 0. Revision Contract

V17.1 does **not delete or silently rewrite the original V17.0 knowledge
claims**.

Instead, every source assertion is assigned to one of three layers:

1.  **NORMALIZED KNOWLEDGE LAYER** --- structurally cleaned
    representation.
2.  **PROVENANCE / ORIGINAL-CLAIM LAYER** --- preserves the original
    V17.0 assertion and wording where material.
3.  **VERIFICATION LAYER** --- records whether a claim is supported,
    requires verification, is ambiguous, or has not been independently
    assessed.

### Epistemic rule

A traditional medicinal-use claim is represented as a **traditional-use
claim**, not automatically as a modern clinical efficacy claim.

A cultural or spiritual claim is represented as **cultural/spiritual
tradition**, not automatically as an empirical biomedical mechanism.

No source claim is silently upgraded.

# 14. Retrieval Safety Rules

``` text
RULE 1:
Never convert TRADITIONALLY_USED_FOR into CLINICALLY_SUPPORTED_FOR.

RULE 2:
Never convert CULTURAL_BELIEF or SPIRITUAL_TRADITION into biomedical mechanism.

RULE 3:
Never present an UNVERIFIED quantitative claim as a verified statistic.

RULE 4:
Never infer historical origin from modern geographic distribution.

RULE 5:
Never merge different botanical species merely because a common-use category is shared.

RULE 6:
Never treat "ancient" or "pre-colonial" as a precise date without chronological evidence.

RULE 7:
Preserve disputed claims in provenance rather than deleting them.

RULE 8:
When a source claim conflicts with independently verified evidence, retain the original claim in provenance and expose the evidence status in the response layer.
```

## Final architectural decision

**V17.0 remains the immutable source layer.**

**V17.1 becomes the normalized semantic layer.**

**Verification does not overwrite either layer.**

The resulting HoloKai stack is:

``` text
V17.0 SOURCE
    ↓
V17.1 NORMALIZATION
    ↓
CLAIM / EVIDENCE GRAPH
    ↓
EMBEDDING CORPUS
    ↓
RAG + KNOWLEDGE GRAPH RETRIEVAL
    ↓
EPISTEMICALLY CONTROLLED HOLOKAI RESPONSE
```

This preserves the cultural corpus while preventing HoloKai from
accidentally turning an unverified traditional claim into a verified
historical or biomedical fact.
